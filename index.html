About the event

GO:COUNTER MAJOR started on one Discord server during a slow summer. No sponsor, no production company — just a group of people who wanted to see who in the community could actually play under pressure.

Squads are trickling in — Retakes and the new rank system are already live in the community mod, finals are streamed online with full casting, no venue, no travel needed.
